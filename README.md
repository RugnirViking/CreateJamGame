Zeroed in is a game where you must move with haste to complete your objectives as enemy artillery is zeroing into your position - be too tardy and face the end of 1812 Overture.

Made for Create Jam Fall 2026 by a few silly neurodivergent sods.

Cambridge dictionary "zero in on something/someone": 
1. to aim a weapon directly at something or someone:

-" Modern military aircraft use computers to help them zero in on their targets. "
